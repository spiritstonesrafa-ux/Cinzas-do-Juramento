# M7-A — Fornalha do Juramento

The `fornalha_*.png` images are original backgrounds generated for this project on 2026-11-12. They depict the mine entrance, hoist shaft, ash gallery, cooling canal, foundry, and sanctuary as separate room-scale views. Their direction follows the project's detailed gothic environment art: layered basalt architecture, oxidized iron, ember light, and cool teal shadows. No characters or text are baked into the backgrounds.

The first `fornalha_bg.png` panoramic plate is retained as the foundry room's background. The earlier all-world stretch that made it appear blurred and empty has been removed. Platforms use the project's masonry architecture atlas, with separate collision and visual layers.
